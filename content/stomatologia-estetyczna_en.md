# Cosmetic dentistry

Cosmetic dentistry aims to improve the appearance of teeth, particularly their colour, shape and proportions. At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, cosmetic treatments include teeth whitening, composite bonding and composite or porcelain veneers.

## Teeth whitening

Two whitening methods are available at the practice:

### Take-home tray whitening

This method uses custom-fitted trays provided by the dentist and a special whitening gel applied at home. The duration of treatment is determined individually according to the patient's needs and the product used.

Treatment can be repeated if necessary. For sensitive teeth, the trays may also be used to apply remineralising products.

### Lamp-assisted whitening

During the procedure, the gums are protected and whitening gel is applied to the tooth surfaces. The result depends on the individual characteristics of the teeth and the type of staining.

## Composite bonding

Composite bonding involves reshaping teeth with modern composite materials designed to closely resemble natural enamel. The material is applied in layers without affecting deeper tooth tissues.

### Who may benefit from bonding?

This option is intended for patients who want to improve their smile quickly without prosthetic or orthodontic treatment. It can be suitable for people with healthy teeth and minor aesthetic concerns such as staining, irregularities or gaps between teeth (diastemas).

### Benefits of bonding

- An immediate aesthetic improvement, even for several teeth.
- A natural appearance without tooth grinding or major changes.
- Treatment often takes just one or two appointments.
- Comfort and a pain-free procedure.

## Veneers

Veneers are thin layers of composite or porcelain permanently attached to the front surfaces of teeth. They improve colour, shape, length and minor irregularities while maintaining a natural appearance.

Two types of veneers are used in cosmetic dentistry:

- **Composite veneers** – made from composite material, allowing the dentist to shape and improve the appearance of teeth.
- **Porcelain veneers** – made from ceramic material matched to the shape and colour of the teeth.

### Who may benefit from veneers?

Veneers may suit patients who want a more comprehensive improvement to their smile. They are recommended for people with staining, uneven teeth, gaps or an unsatisfactory tooth shape, provided there are no major dental problems.

### Benefits of veneers

- Long-term improvement in smile aesthetics.
- Veneers may retain an attractive appearance for many years, depending on the material, oral hygiene and individual circumstances.
- Properly designed veneers can create a beautiful, balanced smile.
