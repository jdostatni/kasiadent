# General dentistry

General dentistry includes the prevention, detection and treatment of tooth decay, as well as the restoration of damaged tooth structure. Its main purpose is to preserve natural teeth and restore their function and appearance.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, general dental care includes diagnostics, individually selected treatments and follow-up assessments.

## Treatments available

General dentistry includes:

- Dental fillings
- Tooth crown restoration
- Temporary fillings
- Medicated dressings
- Improvements to tooth aesthetics

## Modern materials and composite fillings

We use modern dental materials, including composite fillings, to restore damaged tooth structure while matching the natural shape and colour of the tooth.

Composite materials offer good aesthetic properties and suitable mechanical strength, making them widely used in general dentistry.

## Local anaesthesia

Local anaesthesia is used during dental procedures to reduce pain and provide as much comfort as possible.

The type of anaesthesia is selected individually, taking into account the planned procedure and the patient's health.

## On-site dental X-rays

In some cases, general dental treatment requires radiographic imaging beforehand.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, intraoral dental X-rays can be taken on site without referring the patient to another facility for this examination.

Dental X-rays help assess the teeth more accurately and plan suitable treatment.
