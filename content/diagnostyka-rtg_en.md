# Dental X-ray diagnostics

Radiographic imaging is an important part of identifying dental problems and planning and monitoring treatment.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, we have an X-ray unit for taking intraoral dental X-rays on site, without needing to refer patients elsewhere for this type of imaging.

## Uses of dental X-rays

Dental X-rays are used to:

- Detect tooth decay.
- Identify inflammation around the tips of tooth roots.
- Diagnose and monitor endodontic (root canal) treatment.
- Support diagnostics related to oral surgery.
- Check for developing permanent teeth.

## Digital dental X-rays

We use digital radiography, which can reduce radiation exposure several-fold compared with conventional film-based techniques.

Taking an X-ray is quick and painless. Digital images help assess the condition of the teeth and provide information for treatment planning.

## CBCT scanning

When more detailed imaging is required, **dr KASIA DENT** works with an external centre providing cone beam computed tomography (CBCT).

This examination enables three-dimensional assessment of anatomical structures and can help diagnose more complex dental cases.
