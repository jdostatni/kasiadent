# Your first visit

Your first appointment at dr KASIA DENT in Reda is an opportunity to discuss your needs, assess your oral health and explore treatment options. I want everyone who visits my practice to feel comfortable and have time to ask questions and address any concerns.

## What happens during your first visit?

### 1. Booking and registration

You can book by phone or email. If I cannot answer your call, I will call you back as soon as possible.

Before treatment begins, I will ask you to complete a medical questionnaire covering basic health information, medication and any allergies.

### 2. Medical history and dental examination

I begin with a conversation about your health, symptoms and expectations. I then examine your mouth, assessing your teeth, gums and other oral tissues.

### 3. Diagnostics

Where needed, I carry out additional tests, including intraoral dental X-rays on site, to help assess your teeth and plan suitable treatment.

### 4. Discussing your treatment plan

Based on the examination and diagnostic findings, I explain possible treatments, the suggested order of procedures and estimated costs.

I want every patient to understand their oral health and make informed decisions about further treatment.

## How should you prepare for your first visit?

It is helpful to bring:

- Proof of identity.
- A list of your medications, particularly if you are being treated for a chronic condition.
- Previous dental X-rays, if available.
- Information about past illnesses, allergies and previous dental treatment.
- Any relevant medical records you have.

## Your child's first visit

A child's first experience of a dental practice is particularly important. I aim to create a calm, friendly atmosphere suited to each child's age and needs.

We also offer introductory visits, during which children can explore the practice, become familiar with dental equipment and get used to the new surroundings. Building trust and positive associations with dental visits is important to me.
