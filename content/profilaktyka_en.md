# Preventive dentistry

Preventive dental care includes treatments aimed at keeping teeth and gums healthy and preventing decay and periodontal disease. Regular professional cleaning helps remove tartar and deposits, while appropriate preventive treatments help protect enamel.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, we provide preventive treatments including scaling, air polishing and fluoride application.

## Scaling (tartar removal)

Scaling removes hard deposits that most commonly build up around the necks of teeth. Tartar accumulation encourages bacterial growth and gum inflammation. Untreated periodontal disease can lead to loss of the bone surrounding teeth and, in advanced cases, tooth loss.

Tartar is removed using an ultrasonic scaler. The device uses ultrasonic vibrations to break up accumulated deposits.

The cleaned teeth are then polished to make their surfaces smoother, which may help limit new deposits.

## Air polishing

Air polishing is a professional cleaning procedure that also improves the appearance of teeth. It thoroughly removes surface deposits and stains caused by coffee, tea, smoking and other factors.

The air-polishing device can also reach areas that may be difficult to clean thoroughly with a manual or electric toothbrush.

The recommended frequency of professional cleaning is determined individually according to the amount of deposits, gum health and the patient's needs.

## Fluoride treatment

Fluoride application is a preventive dental procedure using products with a high fluoride concentration. Fluoride compounds interact with tooth enamel and calcium compounds, making the enamel less susceptible to acid dissolution. This helps strengthen enamel and increase resistance to bacteria that cause decay.

The main reasons for fluoride treatment include preventing decay in adults and children, promoting enamel remineralisation and reducing sensitivity around exposed tooth necks.

Excessive fluoride exposure can be harmful, so such treatments should be recommended and performed by qualified professionals.
