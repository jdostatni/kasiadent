# About me

**Katarzyna Dostatnia, dentist**

I am a dentist, and at my dr KASIA DENT practice in Reda I treat both adults and children. I graduated in dentistry from the Medical University of Gdańsk in 2007. Since the beginning of my professional career, I have continually developed my skills through numerous courses and training programmes to provide care of the highest possible standard.

### What do I specialise in?

My day-to-day work includes:

- General dentistry
- Microscope-assisted endodontics (root canal treatment)
- Prosthetic dentistry
- Cosmetic dentistry
- Preventive dentistry
- Children's dentistry

I have a particular interest in endodontics, including precise root canal and repeat root canal treatment using a dental microscope. This allows me to work accurately and carry out treatment thoroughly, including in more challenging cases.

Professionalism and attention to detail are central to my work. Empathy, attentiveness and an individual approach to each patient's needs, concerns and expectations are equally important to me. I want my practice to be a calm and trusting environment, where visits are associated with comfort rather than stress. I am pleased that many patients have placed their trust in me and have remained under my care for more than a decade.

### English-speaking patients welcome

I offer consultations and treatment in both Polish and English.
