# Prosthetic dentistry

Prosthetic dentistry focuses on restoring damaged teeth and replacing missing ones. Its aims are to restore chewing function, improve the appearance of the smile and recreate the natural shape of teeth.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, we provide prosthetic restorations including veneers, crowns, bridges and removable dentures. Each restoration is selected individually based on the patient's teeth and needs.

## Veneers

Veneers are thin layers of composite or porcelain permanently bonded to the front surfaces of teeth. They can improve tooth colour, shape, length and minor irregularities while preserving a natural appearance.

Two types of veneers are used in cosmetic dentistry:

- **Composite veneers** – made from composite material that can be shaped to improve the teeth's appearance.
- **Porcelain veneers** – made from ceramic material matched to the shape and colour of the teeth.

## Crowns and bridges

Crowns and bridges are fixed restorations used to rebuild damaged teeth or replace missing ones.

### Dental crowns

A dental crown covers a suitably prepared tooth, restoring its natural shape, function and appearance. It is used, among other situations, when tooth structure is extensively damaged and a conventional filling may not be sufficient.

Crowns may also be used to restore teeth after root canal treatment when their condition requires it.

### Dental bridges

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth. It is a fixed restoration supported by appropriately prepared teeth next to the gap.

A bridge restores missing teeth and improves chewing function and the appearance of the smile.

### Benefits of crowns and bridges

- Restoration of tooth shape and function.
- Replacement of missing teeth.
- Improved smile aesthetics.
- Shape and colour can be tailored to the individual patient.

## Removable dentures

Removable dentures are prosthetic appliances that patients can take out and put back in themselves. They are used when some or all teeth are missing.

Their purpose is to replace missing teeth and improve chewing function and smile aesthetics.

The practice provides the following types of removable dentures:

- **Partial acrylic dentures** – replace missing teeth when some natural teeth remain.
- **Complete acrylic dentures** – used when all teeth are missing from the upper or lower jaw.
- **Cast-metal framework dentures** – partial dentures usually built around a metal framework, providing stability and distributing chewing forces.
- **Flexible dentures (e.g. acetal or nylon)** – made from thermoplastic materials and offered as an alternative to traditional acrylic dentures in selected cases.

The type of denture and how it is made are chosen individually, taking account of the patient's anatomy and needs.
