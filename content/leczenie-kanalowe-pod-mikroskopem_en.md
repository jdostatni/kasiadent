# Microscope-assisted endodontics – root canal treatment

Endodontics is a constantly developing field of dentistry. Modern technologies and precision instruments make it possible to reach, clean, shape and fill hard-to-access root canals.

At **dr KASIA DENT in Reda**, root canal treatment is performed using a dental microscope, allowing accurate work even when root canal anatomy is complex.

## What does root canal treatment involve?

Root canal treatment should be performed using a microscope and radiographic guidance. This provides good visibility and helps the dentist understand the often complex anatomy of canals inside the tooth roots.

A **rubber dam** is used during treatment. This flexible protective sheet isolates the treated tooth from the rest of the mouth. It protects soft tissues from the solutions used to disinfect root canals and helps prevent saliva from entering the tooth.

## Stages of root canal treatment

Root canal treatment includes the following stages:

1. **Opening the pulp chamber and removing the pulp** – removing living or non-vital pulp under local anaesthesia.
2. **Cleaning and shaping the root canals** – mechanically preparing the canal or canals and irrigating them thoroughly for disinfection.
3. **Drying the root canals** – carefully drying the prepared and disinfected canals.
4. **Filling the root canals** – using biocompatible materials to seal the canals.
5. **Restoring the tooth** – rebuilding the tooth after root canal treatment is complete.

## Restoring a tooth after root canal treatment

After root canal treatment, the tooth may need to be restored with a post and core or covered with a prosthetic crown to protect it from fracture.

The appropriate restoration depends on the tooth's condition and how much natural tooth structure remains.
