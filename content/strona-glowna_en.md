## Our services

### [General dentistry](/uslugi/stomatologia-zachowawcza)

Tooth decay treatment, fillings and tooth restorations.

### [Microscope-assisted root canal treatment](/uslugi/leczenie-kanalowe-pod-mikroskopem)

Root canal treatment using a dental microscope.

### [Prosthetic dentistry](/uslugi/protetyka)

Crowns, bridges, veneers and removable dentures.

### [Cosmetic dentistry](/uslugi/stomatologia-estetyczna)

Teeth whitening, composite bonding and veneers.

### [Preventive dentistry](/uslugi/profilaktyka)

Scaling, air polishing and fluoride treatment.

### [Children's dentistry](/uslugi/stomatologia-dziecieca)

Dental check-ups, cavity treatment and introductory visits.

### [Dental X-ray diagnostics](/uslugi/diagnostyka-rtg)

Digital intraoral dental X-rays available on site.
